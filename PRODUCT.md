# Product

## Register

product

## Users

RECTOP operators and marketing managers who maintain a weekly performance report. They enter daily facts, review month and week dynamics, compare cities, sources, messages, and brands, and need shared data to be understandable from any computer.

## Product Purpose

The app turns Google Sheets-backed daily inputs into a shared weekly/monthly dashboard for leads, qualified leads, sales, messages, forecasts, events, sources, and brand performance. Success means data can be entered once in the admin panel and then viewed consistently from any computer through the published site.

## Brand Personality

Clear, focused, operational. The interface should feel like a calm premium dashboard: compact, readable, and aligned with the RECTOP blue glass brand system.

## Anti-references

Do not make the dashboard feel like a marketing landing page, a decorative bento mockup, or a local-only prototype. Avoid mixing `Сообщения` into city totals, hiding save state, oversized cards, unreadable dense labels, or using temporary browser-only data for shared business numbers.

## Design Principles

- Shared source of truth: saved values must live in the shared Google Sheets layer when possible.
- Separation by meaning: `Все` means `МСК + СПБ`; messages and source breakdowns stay in their own views.
- Fast daily entry: inputs should be compact, scannable, and save whole groups of values at once.
- Explain the chart: events, source splits, forecasts, recommendations, and brand deltas should clarify why totals moved.
- Brand restraint: use RECTOP blue, navy, ice surfaces, and simple controls without decorative clutter.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic buttons, labels for form fields, readable contrast, visible focus states, and responsive layouts that remain usable on narrow screens. Avoid relying on color alone for meaning.
